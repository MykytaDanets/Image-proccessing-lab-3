camera.png: Camera photograph, distributed by scikit-image as skimage.data.camera.
Source documentation: https://scikit-image.org/docs/stable/api/skimage.data.html#skimage.data.camera
Camera photograph by Lav Varshney. The scikit-image documentation identifies the image as CC0, with no copyright restrictions.
The image is included locally; scikit-image is NOT required to run this project.
synthetic_scene: original generated experimental image in project.py, with three simple shapes and known drawn boundaries. It is labelled synthetic, not a real photograph.
All derivative images and figures are calculated locally from these inputs.
